MONDE DU 3B — LOCAL 3D EDITION

Card names, fictional setting and supplied illustrations: the 3B project owner.
The 368 models are stylized local Blender adaptations. Characters share reusable
base topology; these files are not photogrammetry or exact replicas of the illustrations.

Human bases, hair, modular clothing and humanoid animation source:
Quaternius, Standard free editions, Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html
https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

3B local adaptations: face morphs, wardrobe assembly, colour roles, ornaments,
country-specific guardians, articulated animal spirits, objects and miniature
architectural interpretations, glTF exports and gameplay integration.

Architectural miniatures are stylized studies, not precise site reconstructions.
No paid asset pack, Fal generation, MetaHuman or DLSS asset is included.
