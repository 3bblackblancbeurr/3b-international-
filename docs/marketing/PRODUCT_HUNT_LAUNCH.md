## Short description
3B International is a web/PWA experience that connects a brand identity with Passeport 3B, a narrative world, manga, games, community features and future physical products.

## Maker story
3B International started as a brand idea around Black • Blanc • Beur and grew into a broader digital ecosystem. The project now connects a public web/PWA application, Passeport 3B, a narrative universe built around eight countries and values, manga, games, community features and creator tools.

The goal is not to put unrelated features into one menu. The goal is to make one identity travel across physical products, digital membership, storytelling and interactive experiences.

The French figurative trademark 3B International is registered with INPI under no. 5258267 for the goods and services covered by its registration.

## Launch discipline
- Launch from a personal maker account.
- Do not ask people directly to upvote.
- Ask interested people to visit, try it and leave useful feedback/comments.
- Prefer launching when the screenshots, install flow and public pages are stable.
- If scheduling ahead, the official Product Hunt guide says 12:01 AM Pacific Time is the preferred start when there are no other constraints.

## Success goals
Primary:
- qualified visits to the official 3B site
- useful comments and UX feedback
- new creator/tech contacts
- mentions/backlinks

Secondary:
- followers
- newsletter/community interest
- partnership leads

Do not define success only by Product of the Day ranking.
